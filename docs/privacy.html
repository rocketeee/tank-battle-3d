中文

萌坦大战是一款卡通坦克射击游戏。当前版本可以离线玩完核心循环：移动、瞄准、射击、波间三选一、结算后再来一局。不需要注册账号。

开发者是个人：Steve Zhou，以 GitHub 用户名 rocketeee 发布。这里没有另外发明公司名称。

现在实际处理什么

游戏把下面这些内容只写在你的设备本地（浏览器 localStorage 或应用私有存储），不会上传到开发者的服务器。开发者目前没有自己的后端。

  • 最佳波次、击杀数、存活时间、已玩局数
  • 金币，以及主炮、装甲、履带、雷达的整备等级（每项最高 20 级，满级后不再变强）
  • 已解锁的涂装和当前涂装（只改颜色，不加伤害）
  • “去除插屏”的本地标记（当前是模拟支付，不是真订单）
  • 瞄准灵敏度、是否看过新手提示或电脑键位提示

卸载应用或清除网站数据后，这些记录会消失。没有账号可删除。

Android 清单里目前只有 INTERNET，给系统 WebView 使用。没有位置、通讯录、相机、麦克风权限。没有广告 SDK，没有支付 SDK，没有统计 SDK。

以后可能加上、现在还没有

如果以后上架并打开商业化，只计划这些，而且都要先经过你的同意或商店的购买界面：

  • 激励广告：每局最多复活 1 次，每局最多换一批升级 1 次。
  • 插屏广告：每两次成功结算最多 1 次，启动时和开火时不出现。购买“去除广告”后不再出现插屏。去除广告不加伤害。
  • 应用内购买：去除广告，以及涂装。涂装只改外观。
  • 广告同意（例如 Google UMP），在真正请求广告之前。

这些功能一旦接上 Google AdMob 或 Google Play 结算，广告网络可能会处理广告 ID、设备信息、IP 地址和广告互动。那是广告服务商的处理，不是本游戏自己的账号系统。具体数据类别以接 SDK 时 Google 的数据安全表为准，会同步改这一页。

不会出售个人数据。不会做付费伤害、抽卡战力或联网对战。

English

萌坦大战 (Tank Battle 3D) is a cartoon tank shooter. The current build plays offline: move, aim, shoot, pick one of three upgrades between waves, then settle and retry. No account is required.

The developer is an individual, Steve Zhou, publishing as the GitHub user rocketeee. This policy does not invent a company name.

What this build actually stores

The game writes the following only on your device (browser localStorage or app private storage). It is not sent to a server run by the developer. There is no first-party backend.

  • Best wave, kills, survival time, and run count
  • Gold, and the level of the main gun, armor, tracks, and radar (20 per slot, then they stop getting stronger)
  • Unlocked paints and the equipped paint (color only, no damage bonus)
  • A local “remove interstitials” flag (a mock purchase, not a real order)
  • Aim sensitivity, and whether the coach or desktop key hint has been seen

Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes these records. There is no account to delete.

The Android manifest currently requests only INTERNET, for the system WebView. There is no location, contacts, camera, or microphone permission. There is no ads SDK, billing SDK, or analytics SDK in this build.

What may be added later, and is not in this build

  • Rewarded ads: at most one revive per run, and at most one upgrade reroll per run.
  • Interstitials: at most one per two successful settles, never at launch and never while firing. A “remove ads” purchase turns interstitials off and does not increase damage.
  • In-app products: remove-ads, and cosmetic paints only.
  • An ads consent form (for example Google UMP) before any real ad request.

If AdMob or Google Play Billing is added, the ad network may process an advertising ID, device information, IP address, and ad interactions. That is the ad provider’s processing, not a game account. The data-safety form and this page will be updated to match the SDK that actually ships. Personal data is not sold. Paid damage, power gacha, and online matches are not part of the product.

Age

The art is cartoon tanks, with no gore and no real-world flags or emblems. Core play does not require login. Whether the Play target audience includes children is a choice the developer must confirm on the store questionnaire. Until that is confirmed, this build does not claim to be directed to children, and it does not serve ads.
